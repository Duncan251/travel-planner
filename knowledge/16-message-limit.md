# I've hit the daily message limit

To keep this free prototype available to everyone, each account can send up to 50 messages a day to Katie. The limit resets at midnight UTC.

Your trip is saved, so you can carry on tomorrow exactly where you left off.

**To get more from each message:** combine several details or requests in one message, for example "We're two adults, mid-range budget, love seafood, and want a relaxed pace."

Messages to Amber, our support assistant, don't count toward this limit.
