# Where do Katie's recommendations come from?

Katie combines three sources:

- **Place information from Google.** Names, addresses, ratings, opening hours and websites of restaurants, cafes, pubs and sights come from Google's places data, looked up when Katie plans your trip.
- **Weather from Open-Meteo**, a weather data service. See "Weather: forecasts and typical conditions."
- **The AI model's general travel knowledge**, used for ideas, routes and how to pace a trip.

Katie only includes a website link if it came from Google's place data, and she shouldn't name a specific business unless she has looked it up. If you ever spot a place that doesn't exist or a link that doesn't work, please report it, because that shouldn't happen.

We don't receive payment for recommending any business.
