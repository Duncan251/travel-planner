# Giving feedback or suggesting a feature

We'd love to hear what works, what doesn't, and what you'd like Katie to do next.

- **Quick feedback:** use the thumbs up or down at the end of a support conversation.
- **Ideas and detailed feedback:** tell the support assistant and ask it to pass your suggestion on.
- **Problems with an itinerary** (a closed restaurant, a wrong link, an odd suggestion): please report them. Every report helps improve Katie's recommendations.

Katie Travel is a prototype, and your feedback directly shapes what gets built next.
