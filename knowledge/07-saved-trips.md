# Finding and continuing a saved trip

All your trips are listed on the **Your trips** page, most recent first. Click **Your trips** at the top of the page, then click any trip to pick up where you left off.

When you return to a trip, Katie reviews a summary of what you've already agreed, including your plans, decisions and any open questions, so you don't need to repeat yourself. She may check whether anything has changed since you last spoke.

**Can't see a trip?**
- Make sure you're signed in with the same email address you used before.

If a trip is still missing, contact us through Amber, our support assistant.
