# How do I sign in?

Katie Travel uses a "magic link" instead of a password.

1. Enter your email address on the welcome page.
2. We email you a sign-in link.
3. Click the link, and you're signed in.

There's no password to create or forget. The same steps create your account the first time and sign you in afterwards.

**Good to know**
- The link works once and expires after 1 hour. If it's expired, just request a new one.
- Open the link on the same device and browser where you requested it, if you can.
- You'll stay signed in on that device until you sign out.

If the email doesn't arrive, see "I didn't receive my sign-in email."
