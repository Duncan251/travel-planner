# Weather: forecasts and typical conditions

- **Trips within the next two weeks:** Katie uses the actual daily forecast for your destination and may suggest swapping days so outdoor plans land on drier ones.
- **Trips further away:** no reliable forecast exists that far ahead, so Katie describes typical conditions for that time of year, based on recent years. These are labelled "typical, not a forecast."

Forecasts change. If your trip is a few weeks away, come back closer to the date and ask Katie to check the weather again.

Weather data comes from Open-Meteo.
