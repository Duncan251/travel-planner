# Who can see my data?

- **Other users can't.** Each account can only see its own preferences and trips. This is enforced in our database, not just by the app.
- **The service operator** can access stored data when needed to provide support, investigate a problem, or keep the service secure.
- **Service providers** process data to make the service work:
  - **Anthropic**, which provides the AI model, receives your messages to generate Katie's replies.
  - **Google** receives the place searches Katie makes (for example, "seafood restaurant near Porto Ribeira"), not your name or email.
  - **Open-Meteo** receives the locations and dates for weather lookups.
  - **Supabase** (database and sign-in) and **Vercel** (website hosting) store and run the service.
