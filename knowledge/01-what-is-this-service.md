# What is Katie Travel, and is Katie a real person?

Katie Travel is an AI travel consultant. You chat with Katie about where you'd like to go, how you like to travel and what you enjoy, and she builds a day-by-day itinerary that fits you.

**Katie is an AI, not a person.** She's designed to feel like talking to a well-travelled friend, but every reply is written by an AI model. If you ever want to speak to a human, ask Amber, our support assistant, and she will pass your message on.

**What Katie can do**
- Plan a trip through conversation, one or two questions at a time
- Check the weather for your dates and suggest changes if rain is likely
- Find real cafes, restaurants, pubs and sights along your route
- Remember your travel preferences and your saved trips

**What Katie can't do**
- Book or pay for anything (see "Why doesn't Katie book things for me?")
- Guarantee that prices, opening hours or availability are current

Katie Travel is a prototype, so expect the occasional rough edge, and please tell us when you find one.
