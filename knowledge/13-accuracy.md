# What should I double-check before I travel?

Katie's suggestions are a starting point, not a guarantee. Before you go, always check directly with the venue or official source:

- **Opening hours and closing days**, especially around public holidays
- **Prices and tickets**, which Katie doesn't quote
- **Whether you need to book**, and booking availability
- **Transport times and timetables**
- **Entry requirements, visas and travel advice** from official government sources

Anything marked **⚑** in your itinerary is something to check or book.

**If something was wrong** (a place had closed, the hours were wrong, a link didn't work), please tell us through Amber, our support assistant. Reports like these help us improve Katie.
