# Renaming or deleting a trip

**To rename a trip:** click **Your trips** at the top of the page, type the new name in the box under the trip, and click **Rename**. Once you've renamed a trip, Katie won't change its name.

**To delete a trip:** on the **Your trips** page, click **Delete** under the trip. You'll be asked to confirm.

Deleting a trip permanently removes the trip, its itinerary and its conversation. It can't be undone.

To delete everything, including your account, see "Deleting your account and data."
