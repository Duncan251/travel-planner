# Understanding your itinerary

Itineraries are designed to be read on a phone while you travel.

- **Trip summary:** dates, where you're staying, the overall pace and typical weather.
- **Day by day:** each day has a short theme, then morning, afternoon and evening suggestions. Times are given only where they matter, such as a timed ticket or a train.
- **"Why you":** a short note on why each suggestion fits what you told Katie.
- **⚑ Flags:** things to act on, such as "⚑ Book ahead" or "⚑ Check opening hours".
- **Rain plan:** an indoor alternative for mostly outdoor days.
- **"If you've got energy":** an optional extra for the end of the day.
- **Before you go:** everything to book or check, most urgent first.

Want something changed? Just ask Katie, for example "swap Day 3 and Day 4" or "find a different dinner spot on Day 2."
