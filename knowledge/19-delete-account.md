# Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account and everything stored with it at any time.

1. While signed in, open the **Privacy** page (linked at the bottom of every page).
2. Click **Delete my account and data**.
3. Type DELETE to confirm, then click **Permanently delete**.

This permanently deletes your account, your preferences, and all your trips, itineraries and conversations. It can't be undone.

If you'd only like to remove one trip, see "Renaming or deleting a trip." To change specific preferences, see "Setting up and changing your travel preferences."
