# Starting a new trip

1. Click **New trip** at the top of the page. (When you first sign in, you land on a new trip, so you can start typing straight away.)
2. Tell Katie roughly what you have in mind. It can be specific ("10 days in northern Portugal in May") or vague ("somewhere warm in March").
3. Katie asks a few questions, one or two at a time, then offers to draft an itinerary.

Your trip is saved automatically as you go. Katie gives it a name based on the destination and dates, which you can change at any time from **Your trips**.

**Tip:** you don't need to have everything decided. Katie is happy to suggest destinations, timings and ideas if you're still exploring.
