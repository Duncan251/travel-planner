# What data do you store about me?

We store only what's needed to run the service:

- **Your email address**, to sign you in
- **Your travel preferences**, if you choose to add them
- **Your trips**: names, itineraries, conversations with Katie, and short summaries used to pick up where you left off
- **Support conversations**, and any requests passed to a person
- **Basic usage information**, such as message counts, to apply daily limits

We don't store payment details, because we never take payments. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Dietary and accessibility details are personal, which is why they're optional, and you can edit or delete them at any time.

See also "Who can see my data?" and "Deleting your account and data."
