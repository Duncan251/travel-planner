# Why doesn't Katie book things for me?

Katie plans your trip but doesn't make bookings or take payments. You book flights, hotels, tours and restaurants directly with the provider.

We've made this choice deliberately. Bookings involve your money and personal details, and we don't think an AI should commit to them on your behalf without careful safeguards. Katie's "Before you go" list tells you exactly what to book and in what order.

We may add help with bookings in future, but always with your explicit approval before anything is confirmed.
