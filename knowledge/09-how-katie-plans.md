# How Katie plans your trip

Katie works like a good travel consultant: she listens first, then plans, then refines.

1. **The basics:** where, when, for how long, who's going and a rough budget.
2. **Your travel style:** pace, guided or self-guided, food, must-sees and things to avoid.
3. **A first draft:** a day-by-day itinerary, followed by a question about what you'd change.
4. **Refining:** once you've reacted to the draft, Katie raises practical points one or two at a time, such as weather, travel times between places, things to book ahead, and days when places may be closed.
5. **The final itinerary:** a clean version with a "Before you go" checklist.

You can jump ahead at any point. Ask for a draft straight away and Katie will make sensible assumptions, tell you what they are, and fill in the gaps afterwards.
