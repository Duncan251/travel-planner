# Setting up and changing your travel preferences

Your travel preferences help Katie plan trips that suit you without asking the same questions every time.

**What you can save**
- Food likes, dislikes and dietary needs
- Guided tours or exploring on your own
- Pace: packed days or plenty of downtime
- Preferred airlines, hotel chains and tour companies
- Interests, and things you'd rather avoid
- Any accessibility needs

Everything is optional. You can skip the preferences page when you first sign in and add details later.

**To change your preferences:** click **Preferences** and edit any field. Changes apply to your next conversation with Katie.

**Saving preferences from a chat:** if you mention something lasting, such as "I've gone vegetarian", Katie may ask whether to save it to your preferences. Nothing is saved unless you say yes.

Things that change from trip to trip, like budget, dates and who's travelling, aren't stored as preferences. Katie asks about those for each trip.
